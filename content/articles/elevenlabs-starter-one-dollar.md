I paid around **₹100** for a month of ElevenLabs. Normally the Starter plan is about ₹600 a month. If you have ever wanted to try a proper AI voice for your videos without committing to anything, this is the cheapest that door has been.

## The offer

On the [ElevenLabs pricing page](https://elevenlabs.io/pricing), the Starter plan is listed as **$1 for the first month, then $6 per month**. With taxes, $1 came to roughly ₹100 on my card.

When I recorded the video on 11 November 2026, the first-month price was running until **16 November**. Offers like this come and go, so check the pricing page for today's number before you get your card out.

## What you get for it

| | Free | Starter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | $0 | $1 first month, then $6 |
| Credits per month | 10,000 | 30,000 |
| Commercial licence | No | Yes |
| Instant voice cloning | No | Yes |
| Dubbing Studio | No | Yes |

**30,000 credits is around half an hour of voiceover.** That does not sound like much until you remember what a Short is: 30 to 60 seconds. Half an hour of finished narration is a lot of Shorts, even after you throw away the takes that sound wrong.

The line that matters most to creators is the **commercial licence**. On the free plan you cannot use the audio in monetised work. On Starter you can.

And yes: that narrator voice in my recent videos is ElevenLabs. This is not a review from the outside. I am using the thing.

## The one catch

After the first month it **renews at $6**. Nothing sneaky, it says so right on the pricing page, but it is easy to forget in four weeks.

So if you only want to try it: **cancel the auto-pay right after you subscribe.** You still keep the full month you paid for. If you love it, you can turn the subscription back on. If you forget about it, nothing happens, which is exactly what you want from a thing you forgot about.

## Is it worth ₹100?

Yes, if any of these are true:

- You make Shorts or Reels and you do not like recording your own voice every time.
- You want to hear what a cloned version of your voice sounds like before deciding whether it belongs in your content.
- You want to dub a video into another language and see if it holds up.

No, if you just want to play with text to speech for five minutes. The free plan's 10,000 credits are fine for that.

## Is it worth ₹600 a month after that?

That depends on how much you publish. My honest test: at the end of the month, count the videos you actually shipped with it. If the answer is more than a handful, ₹600 is cheap next to the time it saved. If the answer is one, cancel and come back when you have a project.
